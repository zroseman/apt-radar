Geographic area

The polygon filters listings after they're scraped — only listings whose coordinates fall inside are kept. If you also leave the Yad2 search URLs blank below, a Yad2 URL gets auto-generated from this polygon's bounding box.

How to set or change the area:

  1. Open geojson.io ↗ in a new tab.
  2. Pan/zoom the map to your area.
  3. Click the polygon tool (pentagon icon, top-right of the map).
  4. Click points on the map to draw your area; double-click the last point to close it.
  5. The right panel shows the full GeoJSON. Copy all of it.
  6. Paste into the "New polygon" field below.

Yad2 search URLs

One Yad2 search URL per line. These tell Apt Radar what to fetch from Yad2. Leave blank to auto-generate one from your geographic polygon above. Price and rooms params get rewritten from your criteria when you save.

How to get a Yad2 URL:

  1. Open yad2.co.il/realestate/rent ↗
  2. Tip: log in first — Yad2 throttles anonymous traffic and may show a captcha.
  3. Set your filters (area, price, rooms) and run the search.
  4. Copy the URL from your browser's address bar.
  5. Paste it below. Add more (one per line) if you want multiple search areas.
